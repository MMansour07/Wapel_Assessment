01 · Brand Concept

Egypt's clearest way to own what you need

Morabh (مُرابِح — "the one who gains") is Egypt's Sharia-compliant Buy Now, Pay Later experience. The identity is built on one promise: nothing hidden, nothing unclear. Every visual decision expresses trust, transparency and steady financial progress — not corporate banking, not decorative religiosity, not crypto hype.

02 · The Symbol — "The Rising Meem"

م + M + growth, in one continuous gesture

The symbol fuses three ideas into a single stroke:

1. The round head of م (meem) — the first letter of مُرابِح.
2. An ascending Latin M for Morabh, whose second peak rises higher than the first — each installment is a step, and the journey ends higher than it began.
3. The floating gold dot: the damma (ُ) that crowns مُ, and the moment of gain — the benefit that Sharia-compliant finance shares fairly.

03 · Wordmarks

One brand, two scripts

The English wordmark is set in customized Manrope ExtraBold — lowercase, open, human. The Arabic wordmark is drawn from Cairo SemiBold — a contemporary Egyptian sans with correct proportions, outlined and refined so the two scripts share weight, rhythm and rounded terminals. The gold diacritics (damma & kasra) are part of the wordmark and must not be removed: they carry the pronunciation مُرابِح and the brand's accent color.

10 · Usage Rules

Clear space & minimum sizes

Keep a clear space equal to the height of the symbol's ring (the meem head) on all sides of any lockup. Nothing enters this zone — no text, edges, or other marks.

AssetMinimum digitalMinimum print
Symbol alone16 px height5 mm
Horizontal lockup24 px height8 mm height
Stacked lockup64 px height18 mm height
Bilingual lockup28 px height10 mm height

Backgrounds

On light backgrounds use the full-color logo. On Morabh Green, Deep Nile or photography, use the white (reverse) logo — the gold damma dot stays gold. Use pure black only for single-color print.

Don't

Stretch or squash
Recolor
Rotate
Add effects or shadows
Place on busy patterns
Low-contrast backgrounds

Also never: remove the gold dot, swap the dot color, separate the diacritics from the Arabic wordmark, re-typeset the wordmarks in system fonts, or enclose the logo in unapproved shapes.
